Our Foundation

OUR STORY

Monte Napoleone was founded in Treviso, Italy, in 1974 with one simple belief:

Quality never goes out of style.

More than fifty years later, that belief still guides every decision we make. Today, Monte Napoleone brings together the strengths of three traditions.

Italian Heritage
Gives us an appreciation for timeless design and elegance.
Scandinavian Thinking
Inspires simplicity, functionality and honesty.
Vietnamese Craftsmanship and Manufacturing Expertise
Allow us to transform ideas into products with precision and consistency.

We respect tradition. But we also believe craftsmanship should continue to evolve. We believe the best shirts are created when experience, ideas and craftsmanship come together with a shared commitment to quality.

Expertise

WHY WE SPECIALIZE

We made a conscious decision. Instead of producing everything, we chose to focus on what we do best: shirts, polo shirts and T-shirts. Real expertise comes from specialization. Over decades, our team has refined the craft of shirtmaking through international manufacturing experience and by meeting the quality standards of demanding brands and retail partners around the world. That experience is reflected in every Monte Napoleone shirt today.

Working closely with our own manufacturing allows us to oversee every stage of production, from fabric selection and pattern development to cutting, sewing, pressing, quality control and final packaging. Before a Monte Napoleone shirt reaches you, it has already been carefully inspected, assessed and refined by the people who made it. Some fabrics are offered as Pre-Order, giving customers access to a wider selection of carefully chosen materials beyond our regular stock.

Experience is not measured by how many products are made. It is measured by the knowledge, precision and pride of the people who make them.
